# Clinical Narrative

**Patient:** Laura Synthetic — female, born 1989-07-30 (`Patient/pat-004`).

Laura is a 36-year-old woman with **hypothyroidism** (diagnosed 2025,
`Condition/cond-hypothyroid`). She has a documented **penicillin allergy**
(`AllergyIntolerance/allergy-pcn`), recorded 2018-03-22 with **criticality
high**: she developed generalized urticaria and angioedema after oral
amoxicillin in 2018 (a **severe** reaction).

Her ongoing chronic medications, started at a primary care visit on
**2025-10-01** (`Encounter/enc-020`), are **levothyroxine 50 mcg once daily**
(`MedicationRequest/mr-levothyroxine`) and **cetirizine 10 mg as needed**
(`MedicationRequest/mr-cetirizine`).

At an acute care visit on **2026-05-22** (`Encounter/enc-021`) for
**streptococcal pharyngitis** (`Condition/cond-pharyngitis`), she was prescribed
**amoxicillin 500 mg three times daily** (`MedicationRequest/mr-amoxicillin`)
and **ibuprofen 400 mg as needed** (`MedicationRequest/mr-ibuprofen`).

Amoxicillin is a penicillin-class beta-lactam antibiotic. Ordering it for a
patient with a documented high-criticality penicillin allergy is a critical
allergy–medication conflict.
